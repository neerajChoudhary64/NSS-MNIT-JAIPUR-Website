The National Service Scheme (NSS) is a premier flagship youth program under the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India.

Launched on September 24, 1969—the centenary year of Mahatma Gandhi—NSS aims to inculcate social responsibility and provide students with direct hands-on experience in community service.

NSS at MNIT Jaipur

At Malaviya National Institute of Technology Jaipur, the NSS Unit represents a vibrant collective of over 500 dedicated student volunteers and esteemed faculty program officers. We synergize technical education with social empathy, tackling environmental degradation, public health, educational inequality, and rural challenges.

The profound motto "NOT ME, BUT YOU" embodies the essence of democratic living and upholds the need for selfless service. It reflects the conviction that the welfare of an individual is ultimately dependent on the welfare of society as a whole.

Our Mission

To foster social consciousness and civic leadership in young minds through active community involvement, disaster mitigation, and educational outreach.

Our Vision

To build an empathetic, socially responsible generation of engineers and leaders who champion environmental sustainability, equity, and holistic national progress.

Core Objectives of NSS MNIT Jaipur

Identify the real-world needs and challenges of the surrounding community and formulate sustainable solutions.
Develop among student volunteers a strong sense of civic competence and social responsibility.
Bridge the gap between higher technical institutions and rural/semi-urban communities.
Cultivate leadership traits, crisis-management abilities, and democratic decision-making.
Mobilize emergency assistance, disaster relief, and voluntary blood donation drives during crises.
Champion national campaigns like Swachh Bharat Abhiyan, Mission LiFE, and Digital Literacy.

Major Flagship Initiatives

Project Prerna

A self-sustaining evening educational model offering tutoring, digital awareness, and life skills to children of campus laborers and underprivileged families.

Green MNIT Model

Decentralized organic composting, botanical geo-tagging, and single-use plastic ban transforming the 317-acre campus into a biodiversity hub.

LifeSaver Blood Registry

A 24x7 verified donor emergency network coordinating urgent platelet and rare blood group donations for government hospitals across Jaipur.

Gram Utthan Rural Cell

Continuous development interventions in adopted villages providing solar lighting guidance, rainwater recharging, and health diagnostics.
